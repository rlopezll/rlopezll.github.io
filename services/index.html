Services

Technical Services

CamperForge collaborates with teams that need experienced technical support during prototyping or production. The service covers the conversion of design ideas into playable systems, the diagnosis of difficult technical problems and the development of foundations that let a game grow with confidence.

The service is grounded in more than 21 years of professional game development across PC, console and mobile platforms.

The main areas of expertise include:

  • Unreal Engine 4 and 5
  • Gameplay and engine programming
  • Modern C++ and C#
  • Multiplayer networking and client/server architecture
  • Performance and CPU optimisation
  • Tools development
  • Technical leadership
  • PC, console and mobile development
